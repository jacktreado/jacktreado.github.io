About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Dresden, Germany. I work at the Max Planck Institute for the Physics of Complex Systems (MPI-PKS) with Frank Jülicher, and in the Cluster of Excellence Physics of Life (PoL) with Otger Campàs.

I build computational models of complex tissues, both for purely theoretical purposes and in collaboration with experimentalists. I am generally interested in the interface between soft condensed matter, active matter, and biology.

In my PhD with Corey O'Hern, I studied packing and jamming in protein cores, multicellular systems, and plant organs. You can read my thesis here.
