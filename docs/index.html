About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Dresden, Germany. I work with Frank Jülicher in the Biological Physics group, and with Otger Campàs at the Physics of Life Cluster of Excellence at TU Dresden. I use computational physics to build models of complex, disordered biological systems, from packing in the cores of single protein molecules to jamming and collective phenomena in multicellular tissues. My work blends soft condensed matter, statistical physics, image analysis and computational geometry.
